# Phase 4 — Self-Deployment (Launch and Build-in-Public Plan)

Goal: a dated 30/60/90-day execution roadmap whose only objective is first
revenue, with a build-in-public engine attached. Dates are real calendar
dates anchored to a kickoff date (ask none — default kickoff is the Monday
following report delivery; state that assumption).

## MVP scope — ruthless cut to first revenue

Apply the test to every proposed feature: **could a customer pay this week
without it?** If yes, cut it from the MVP. Prefer, in order:

1. **Concierge/manual-first** — deliver the outcome by hand before building
   software; the founder is the product.
2. **Assembled** — no-code/off-the-shelf glue (forms, spreadsheets, Zapier,
   an LLM in the loop) delivering the outcome end to end.
3. **Built** — custom software, only for the parts 1 and 2 cannot do.

The report states the MVP as a one-sentence promise ("For [customer], we
will [outcome] by [mechanism] for [price]") plus the shortest step list from
today to a paying customer. Everything else goes in an explicit "deferred
until revenue" list.

## Excuse traps — skip list (include verbatim in the report, adapted to the venture)

Named things founders do instead of selling. The report lists them as
explicitly skipped, with the trigger condition that would un-skip each:

- Logo, brand identity, brand voice document → skip until 10 customers
- Custom domain perfection / website redesign loops → a Carrd page sells fine
- Incorporation before revenue → skip **where legally viable**; flag
  jurisdictions or regulated activities (finance, health, food, children's
  products) where entity/licensing must precede selling — check the intake
  geography and say which applies
- Credentials, certifications, courses "to be ready" → the market is the
  credential
- CRM / tool-stack setup beyond a spreadsheet → skip until the sell
  repeatable's scaling trigger fires
- Perfect pricing page / tiering matrix → one price, adjusted per Phase 2
- Business cards, merch, launch video → no
- Waiting for the "full" product before charging → concierge it

## Build-in-public engine

- **Channel strategy:** one primary channel, chosen where the Phase 1
  audience actually is (X/Twitter, LinkedIn, a subreddit, a niche community,
  YouTube/shorts). Justify the choice; one secondary channel maximum for
  repurposing.
- **Content calendar:** a concrete weekly cadence (default 3 posts/week)
  mapped to four archetypes rotated: (1) progress — what shipped;
  (2) numbers — real metrics including bad ones; (3) lesson — what a test or
  customer taught you; (4) ask — a question that recruits testers or
  customers. Provide the first two weeks of posts as actual drafted topics
  tied to this venture's roadmap.
- **Fix-it-out-loud protocol:** when a user reports a problem or a public
  criticism lands — acknowledge publicly within 24 h, state the fix and
  date, ship it, close the loop publicly with the reporter credited. Every
  such loop is a content asset; log them. Never litigate criticism publicly;
  fix or decline with reasons, once, politely.

## Pricing and first-10-customers plan

- **Pricing:** commit to the Phase 2 winning anchor as the launch price
  (founding-customer discount allowed, time-boxed and honest). No free tier
  at validation stage; free pilots only with a signed conversion date.
- **First 10 customers, in priority order:**
  1. Phase 2 captured emails — personal note within 48 h of launch decision.
  2. Direct founder outreach into the exact Phase 1 communities where pain
     was found (participate first; sell per community norms).
  3. Warm network mapped against the target segment — asks for intros, not
     favors.
  4. Build-in-public audience converts passively; do not count on it for
     the first 10.
- Weekly outreach quota sized to the sell repeatable's time budget; each of
  the first 10 gets a founder conversation logged as research.

## 30/60/90 roadmap structure

Deliver as a dated table, weekly milestones, each week having one primary
milestone and an owner (founder vs AI-assisted per Phase 3):

- **Days 1–30 — Sell the promise:** run/refresh the Phase 2 test, launch the
  concierge MVP, convert the captured list, first paying customer target
  ~day 21–30.
- **Days 31–60 — Deliver and systemize:** serve the first customers by hand,
  run the Phase 3 cadence, harvest delivery pain into product scope, reach
  10 paying customers.
- **Days 61–90 — Decide with data:** raise price or widen the funnel, check
  which repeatables hit scaling triggers, and make the 90-day call —
  double-down (build the "built" layer), pivot (evidence redirected), or
  wind down (thresholds unmet — reference the Phase 2 kill discipline).

Include a milestone table with real dates, and a "week 13 retrospective"
entry that forces the 90-day decision onto the calendar.

## Deliverable

The dated roadmap table (weekly milestones with dates), MVP promise + cut
list, excuse-trap skip list with un-skip triggers, channel strategy with a
two-week drafted content calendar, fix-it-out-loud protocol, and the
first-10-customers plan with quotas.
